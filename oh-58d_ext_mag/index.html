External .50 Cal Magazine for OH-58D

  • 550 round capacity
  • Dual-wall honeycomb construction for damage tolerance and durability
  • Mounts to sidewall of the aircraft

Description

This side mounted magazine has flown in battle for nearly three decades. It has withstood the abuse of debris strikes outside, and internal wear and tear from ripping out heavy .50 cal rounds at 600 rounds per minute. The technology developed for this proven magazine is used in our newer magazines as well.
