Description

The AH-6 OMS has been in combat since 1989 with the U.S. Army’s 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment Little Bird (MD-530) fleet. Most of the original equipment remains in service despite many years of hard battle use. Weapons in use are the M134/GAU-2 7.62mm Gatling Gun, GAU-19 .50 cal Gatling Gun, M260 7-Shot 2.75-inch Rocket Pod, and HELLFIRE missiles. Over 50 units were sold to the U.S. Army and are still in operation today.
