Articulated Display Unit Mount for Black Hawk Cabin

  • Tool-less - articulated and adjustable without tools
  • Stows away - out of the way when not in use, low profile
  • Stable - for easy viewing, doesn't droop during harsh vibration
  • Proven - over 50 units in operation for over 7 years
  • Qualified by the U.S. Coast Guard
  • Complete list of specifications

Description

The Display Unit (DU) Mount attaches to the interior wall of the helicopter in the rear of the cabin. The installation is quick and easy. The articulating joint does not rely on friction, thus preventing the display from drooping. It swivels on two axes to allow the rescue personnel to position the display where it is most ergonomic. The system is qualified and flying on the U.S. Coast Guard Jay Hawk fleet.
