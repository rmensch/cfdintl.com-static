Description

The original MH-6 OMS has been in operation since 1989 with the U.S. Army’s 160th Special Operations Aviation Regiment (SOAR) Little Bird (MD 530) fleet. Most of the original equipment remains in service despite many years of hard battle use. Over 50 units were sold to the U.S. Army and are still in operation today.
