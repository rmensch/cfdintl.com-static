Palletized Gun Mount System (PGMS)


  • PGMS Low Ceiling

  • PGMS High Ceiling

  • Universal cargo floor interface

  • Pivots to allow weapon maintenance and ingress/egress

Overview

CFD’s Palletized Gun Mount System (PGMS) is designed to fit in the door/window or on the ramp of a multitude of aircraft. The pallet - the only custom component - attaches to the cargo tie down rings and seat studs. All the rest of the components attach to the pallet. By designing the system this way, most of the components can be stocked in advance and sold off the shelf, cutting cost and lead time substantially.

PGMS comes in two variants depending on the height of the aircraft ceiling.

Features & Benefits

  • Pivoting - the arm that supports the weapon pivots to allow retraction of the weapon into the cabin for maintenance, to aid ingress/egress and to allow the aircraft doors to be closed for reduced drag.
  • Increased range - the arm is compatible with .50 cal weapons to provide an increased Weapons Engagement Zone (WEZ).
  • High Capacity Magazines - 3,000 rounds of 7.62mm and 600 rounds of .50 cal ammunition per magazine. The pallet can be customized to add as many magazines as will fit in the aircraft for even more ammunition.
  • Quick release - the entire system can be removed quickly, without tools.
