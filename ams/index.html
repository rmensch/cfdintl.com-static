Armament Management System

The CFD Armament Management System (AMS) is comprised of an Armament Control Unit (ACU), Armament Panel Switch (APS), and two Rocket and Gun Interface Units (RGIU). This lightweight system is an affordable solution to stores management on both rotary and fixed wing platforms. The AMS was designed from extensive experience in systems integration, internal software development and weapons and fire control solutions. The flexible design enables rapid integration and future upgrade capabilities to sensors, avionics and weapons.


Standard Features

  • Remote or crew-served weapon control
  • Real-time inventory status
  • Remote weapon charging capability
  • Laser activation
  • Misfire detection
  • MIL-STD-1553B and RS-422 serial communication intra-system interface
  • USB mission upload/download
  • Light-weight modular design
  • Jettison control of store stations
  • Night Vision Goggle (NVG) compatibility

Optional Features

  • FLIR video display
  • Counter Measures Dispensing System (CMDS) control
  • Control of laser guided rockets through a laser designation capable FLIR

Weapon Compatiblility

  • M260 Lightweight 7-shot Rocket Launcher (M433 and M439 Fuze Compatible)
  • M261 Lightweight 19-shot Rocket Launcher (M433 and M439 Fuze Compatible)
  • LWL-12 Lightweight 12-shot Rocket Launcher (M433 and M439 Fuze Compatible)
  • M2/M3 Series .50 cal Machine Gun
  • M134 7.62mm Gatling Gun
  • M240 7.62mm Machine Gun

Description

Our AMS will work with any aircraft as either an integrated or federated system. It is designed to meet the most common customer needs without the highest end features to keep the price affordable.
