Searchlight Mount for the Trakkabeam A800

  • Qualified by the U.S. Coast Guard
  • Proven - over 50 in operation for over 7 years
  • Float bag compatible
  • Quick release - rapidly install or remove the spotlight in the field
  • No costly modifications
  • Safe location - Improves hard landing survivability over nose mount/belly mount

Description

The Trakkabeam A800 Searchlite SSM mounts to the sponson and provides a rigid, stable platform for mounting a searchlight. The system is qualified and flying on the U.S. Coast Guard Jay Hawk fleet.
