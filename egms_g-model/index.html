Description

The G-Model EGMS is our most battle proven EGMS. As a search and rescue helicopter, cabin space for crew and patients is critical. EGMS allows the Pave Hawk to carry armament without sacrificing cabin space. Currently, the Air Force operates over 100 EGMS equipped Pave Hawks.

Mechanical installation takes approximately 24 man-hours.
